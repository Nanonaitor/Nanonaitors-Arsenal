# 0.1.0-beta.7

- Corrected beta.6's overly broad offhand restriction. Ball & Chain normal left-click winding and release-to-throw work with an item in the offhand.
- Only right-click acceleration requires an empty offhand. Filling the offhand stops acceleration without canceling the charge.
- Actual shield guarding/dashing still exclusively owns attack input, preventing simultaneous weapon activation.
- Replaced Tartsy's explicit sword-sweep dash sound with the vanilla shield-block sound.
- Kept beta.6's world-space Ball & Chain renderer unchanged.
