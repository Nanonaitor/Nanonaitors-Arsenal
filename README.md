# Nanonaitor's Arsenal

Nanonaitor's Arsenal is a weapon mod built around specialized weapons with special mechanics each.

Note the main Branch is for Minecraft 26.1.2. Check other branches for other versions.
Currently, Minecraft 1.12.2 is planned for a future port/update.

## Included equipment

Every weapon is available in wood, stone, copper, gold, iron, diamond, and
netherite tiers. Copper uses vanilla Copper tool properties. Netherite weapons
upgrade from their matching Diamond weapon with a Netherite Upgrade Smithing
Template and Netherite Ingot.

### Morning Stars

Slow, heavy maces with long spikes. Fully charged hits give Armor
Fracture, reducing armor by 20% per level. Tier caps depend on material, 40% for wood to
100% for diamond. The effect lasts 30 seconds on mobs and 10 seconds on players.

### Scimitars

Fast, curved blades inspired by Middle Eastern swords. Every hit inflicts Weakness II for 10 seconds.

### Claws

Two wrist-mounted weapons controlled independently with left and right click.
Fully charged alternating hits ignore invulnerability frames. Equipping a claw will automatically equip a temporary copy in the off-hand.

### Flails

Hold attack to swing an iron chain and tier-colored head in a 4-block area around
the user every 25 ticks.

### Battering Rams

Two-handed charge weapons that rush forward and smash a tier-dependent t3x3 path through terrain. Higher tiers break harder materials.
Charging consumes hunger, requires an empty offhand, and uses durability when breaking blocks or hitting mobs.

### Balls and Chains

Hold left-click to swing/charge, then release to throw the ball.
The ball hits every swing for x0.5 base damage and the throw/return hit as well. Each charge adds 4
blocks of reach. Fully charged throws gain tier-scaled armor piercing and permanently reduce mob armor.

### Sun-War Bulwark

A massive two-handed shield that can guard all attacks. Its
normal strike deals 1 damage + user armor points. Attacking while
shielding activates a 4-block AOE (deactivates shield and goes on cooldown).
Being so heavy it reduces speed to 40% and shielding to 75%. Inspired by El Pípila (Mexico).

## Enchanting

Arsenal weapons accept appropriate vanilla melee enchantments with the exception of
Sweeping Edge being intentionally restricted to Scimitars. The Sun-War Bulwark accepts
compatible shield enchantments.

## Compatibility

This 26.1.2 release is fully standalone and has no required content-mod dependency.

Release JARs are created under `build/libs/`.
